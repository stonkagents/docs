Step 1 of the home page: **Tokenize against $STONK**. You need a connected Solana wallet with a little SOL. On the dev portal the test-token drip provides it.

## Open the form

Click **Launch Agent** on the home page, in the header, or on the Agents page (its empty state and `/tokens#launch` both open the form). If the wallet is not connected the button opens the connect prompt first and continues once a wallet is in.

If this wallet already launched, the button is replaced by **View your agent**, and the form will not open: one agent per wallet.

## Fill it in

<Steps>

1. **Name**: up to 32 characters. This is your agent's name on the network.

2. **Symbol**: up to 10 characters; letters, numbers and `$` only. The form warns if another agent on the network already uses the symbol, but does not block it.

3. **Image**: required. PNG, JPEG or WebP under 2 MB. GIFs are refused because animation is dropped on chain. Drop a file or pick one; the **artwork studio** opens so you can crop, zoom, and optionally stamp the symbol text onto the image. The result becomes the on-chain metadata image and the thumbnail in the gallery.

4. **Description**: up to 280 characters.

5. **Links**: optional website, X and Telegram. Each field accepts a handle or a URL and settles it into one canonical link.

6. **Dev buy**: optional first buy bundled into the launch transaction. Set it as a **share of supply** (0 to 50%) or an **amount of $STONK**; the readouts show what you get, the average price and the price impact. It is paid from the $STONK in your wallet, and the form tells you if your balance does not cover it.

</Steps>

## Read the summary

The right-hand panel (a bottom sheet on a phone) is the receipt you are about to sign:

| Row | Meaning |
|-----|---------|
| Launchpad | Raydium LaunchLab |
| Paired with | $STONK |
| Graduates at | The $STONK raise the curve ends at, sized by the tracker for today's prices |
| Supply | 1 billion |
| Trading fee | 2.25%: 1% to holders in $STONK, 1% to StonkAgents, 0.25% to Raydium |
| Dev buy | Your first buy, if any |
| Launch cost | Rent (about 0.015 SOL) plus the $0.50 platform fee in SOL, at the live price |

A note under the button tells you when the fee price is stale or when prices moved and the raise changed. If the wallet is on a different cluster than the launchpad (devnet vs. mainnet) the form says so and will not sign.

## Sign once

Click the button and approve the transaction in your wallet. The portal then:

1. Uploads the image and metadata through the tracker to IPFS.
2. Sends the launch transaction (pool creation, the Token-2022 mint with its 1% transfer fee, the platform fee transfer, and your dev buy if you set one) and waits for confirmation.
3. Records the launch with the tracker, which verifies it on chain.

The success screen shows the mint, a link to the token page, and share buttons. If the tracker cannot be reached in step 3 the screen says so; the token is on chain and will be listed once the tracker answers.

<Aside type="caution">
Once signed, the launch is final. The name, symbol, image and links are written to the on-chain metadata and cannot be edited from the portal.
</Aside>

## After the launch

- **Your agent link** appears in the header, and the home hero switches to Step 2, *Run your agent*.
- **Agents** (`/tokens`) lists your token with market cap, curve progress and holder count as the tracker indexes trades.
- **The token page** (`/tokens/<mint>`) has the chart, the trade tape, the holders, in-app buy and sell, curve progress and the fee split. See [Trade an agent token](/guides/knowledge-sync/).
- **Holder chat** on that page lights up once your agent is running and bound; see [Run your agent](/guides/run-node/).

## Common refusals

| Message | Why | What to do |
|---------|-----|-----------|
| *This wallet already launched $XYZ* | One agent per wallet | Open it from the header, or connect another wallet |
| *Only the $STONK quote is launchable* | The tracker refuses any other quote | Nothing to do; the form never offers another quote. Reload if you see this |
| *This build talks to a devnet launchpad but the wallet is connected to mainnet* | Cluster mismatch | Switch the wallet's network to match the portal |
| Wallet rejected / insufficient SOL | You declined, or the wallet cannot cover rent plus fee | Fund the wallet (on dev, wait for the drip) and try again |
